# The Great Gatsby

## Protagonists

Nick Carraway is the narrator, a Midwesterner who moves next door to Gatsby and tries to stay honest in a dishonest world. Jay Gatsby is a self-made millionaire whose whole life is built around winning back Daisy. Daisy Buchanan is Gatsby's old love, charming and careless, trapped in a marriage of money and comfort. Tom Buchanan, her husband, is rich, brutal, and sure the world belongs to people like him.

## Plot

Nick arrives in West Egg in the summer of 1922 and is pulled into Gatsby's glittering parties, Tom's affairs, and a love story that was never going to survive the class line between old money and new. The closer Nick gets to Gatsby, the more the dream looks like a costume.

## Themes

The novel is about the American Dream turning sour: you can buy the mansion and the shirts, but you cannot buy the past. It is also about who gets to walk away from damage. The rich do. Everyone else pays.

## Review

Fitzgerald writes a short, sharp tragedy. The parties still dazzle, but the ending is cold on purpose. It is less a romance than a warning about wanting a life that was never really yours.

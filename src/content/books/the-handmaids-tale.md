# The Handmaid's Tale

## Protagonists

Offred is the narrator, a handmaid whose real name is stolen. She survives by noticing everything. The Commander is the man of the house, powerful in Gilead and hungry for forbidden small freedoms. Serena Joy is his wife, who helped build the ideology and now lives inside its cruelty. Nick is a Guardian whose loyalties are never fully clear. Moira is Offred's friend, braver in public, a reminder of the life before. Ofglen is another handmaid, and a first glimpse of resistance.

## Plot

In Gilead, fertile women are assigned to elite households. Offred walks to the shops, waits for the Ceremony, and tells us the past in pieces: a husband, a child, a job, a name. Small rebellions never feel safe. The ending refuses to comfort you.

## Themes

Atwood is writing about how quickly rights can be taken if enough people call it order. The book is about women's bodies as state property, but also about language, friendship, and the stories we tell to stay human. Complicity is everywhere: wives, aunts, neighbors.

## Review

Cold, precise, and hard to shake. Offred's voice is the engine, not a speech but a whispered record. It reads like a warning that already knows it might be ignored. Still one of the most effective dystopias because so little in it feels invented.
